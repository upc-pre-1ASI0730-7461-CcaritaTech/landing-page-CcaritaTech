Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2025

1. Introduction

At IoBuild, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect information we obtain from our users.

By using our services, you agree to the practices described in this policy. We recommend reading this document carefully and contacting us if you have any questions.

2. Information We Collect

We collect different types of information to provide and improve our services:

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Registration data: name, email, company, phone number
  • Billing information: payment details and billing address
  • Project information: construction details and device configurations
  • Communications: messages you send us through support or contact forms

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • IoT device data: status, energy consumption, configurations
  • Usage data: platform interactions, features used
  • Technical information: IP address, browser type, operating system
  • Cookies and similar technologies to improve user experience

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our IoT platform services
  • Process transactions and send related confirmations
  • Respond to your inquiries and provide technical support
  • Send service updates, security notifications, and administrative notices
  • Analyze platform usage to improve functionalities
  • Detect, prevent, and address fraud, security, or technical issues
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. We share information only in the following circumstances:

4.1 Service Providers

We share information with providers who help us operate our business, such as hosting services, payment processing, data analysis, and technical support. These providers are contractually obligated to protect your information.

4.2 Legal Requirements

We may disclose information if required by law, court order, legal process, or to protect our rights, property, or safety, or those of our users.

4.3 Business Transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

5. Data Security

We implement technical, administrative, and physical security measures designed to protect your information:

  • End-to-end encryption of data in transit and at rest
  • Multi-factor authentication for account access
  • Continuous system monitoring to detect vulnerabilities
  • Strict role-based access controls
  • Regular security audits and penetration testing
  • Ongoing staff training in security best practices

However, no Internet transmission or electronic storage system is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

IoT device data is retained for 24 months from collection. Billing data is maintained according to legal accounting requirements. You may request deletion of your data at any time, subject to our legal obligations.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: Request a copy of the information we have about you
  • Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal information
  • Portability: Receive your data in a structured, readable format
  • Objection: Object to the processing of your personal information
  • Restriction: Request limitation of processing your data

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@iobuild.com. We will respond to your request within 30 days.

8. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience on our platform:

  • Essential cookies: necessary for basic platform functionality
  • Preference cookies: remember your settings and preferences
  • Analytics cookies: help us understand how the platform is used
  • Marketing cookies: used to display relevant content

You can control cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling certain cookies may affect platform functionality.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are directed to construction companies and are not designed for individuals under 18 years of age. We do not intentionally collect information from minors. If we discover we have collected information from a minor, we will delete it immediately.

10. International Transfers

Your information may be transferred and processed on servers located outside your country of residence. We ensure that such transfers comply with applicable data protection laws, including the use of approved standard contractual clauses.

11. GDPR and LGPD Compliance

We comply with the European Union's General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Peru's Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 29733). If you have questions about compliance, contact us.

12. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the new policy on our website and updating the "last updated" date.

We recommend reviewing this policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information.
